01 / PROFILE

BUILT ON CURIOSITY.
DRIVEN BY QUALITY.

Gaming is where I practise observation, explore systems, and pay attention to how player actions meet game rules. This portfolio brings my gaming familiarity together with a structured, honest approach to learning QA.

I focus on clear test steps, observable results, evidence capture, and reports that another tester can reproduce.
